# sort-bins: the reader files each case into a category by the cue that decides it, not by surface
family: problem
elements: stem, items, exhibit
differs: each item goes into exactly one of two or more named bins by the cue that decides it, so the moves are mutually exclusive filings. select-all cannot force this: it has one rule and a yes or no per item

## Why it works
Each item is a new case the reader assigns to a bin, so one idea is applied repeatedly with different surfaces. It prevents lists of examples the unit already walked through (card-new-case). The bins are the unit's own distinctions, defined before the card.
The why for an item explains why it is not the bin its surface suggests (card-why).

## Parts
- stem: one sorting question; the bins are named in the stem or the card frame.
- items: `bins` and 6 to 8 items `{text, why}`; each bin gets at least one item and some item has a tempting wrong bin.
- exhibit: per item, when the sorting cue is visual, so the reader sorts what they see.

## Examples
Source book: the UX book (ids are its card ids or unit and line).
- task-per-sort (u-percept): eight screen fragments sorted by the cue that makes the elements look grouped (gap, colour, border, line, background band). Essential content: each fragment is a new situation, with the cue stated only in the misplaced-why.
- task-per-vars (u-percept): chart and screen fragments sorted by the visual variable that carries the value. Essential content: filled-in fragments in which position, size, tone or outline carries it.
- task-compose-owner (u-compose): nine cases of generated screens sorted by who owns the layout. Essential content: the bins are the three owners from the section.
Elsewhere: Eight clinic notes sorted into "symptom", "finding", "plan".

## Compose and vary
- Combines with: select-all, order (after sorting, rank within a bin).
- Vary: choose bins that split on a cue, not on topic; keep each bin non-empty and avoid one-to-one pairing with an obvious keyword.
- Usual failure: bins differ by topic words in the item, so the reader sorts by vocabulary.

## Seed
Can each item be filed only by applying the idea, and does each item come from a case the unit has not worked?
