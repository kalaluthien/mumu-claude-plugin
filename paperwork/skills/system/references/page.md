# page

Show a checked contract as one HTML page a person reads, searches and plays without leaving its diagram.

## Domain

- **page**: one `.html` file made by `make-page.py` from a contract. The contract is its page data, and each node, edge and area is an anchor, so it follows the page rules of [relay.md](relay.md).
- **diagram**: one level drawn to fill the window: each node a rectangle with its label and its tag's name, near-square but a `state`, well rounded as a statechart draws it, and a `dataStore`, a cylinder; each area a frame, dashed for a `region` or a `trustZone`, and a `lane` or `phase` a band with no frame, the bands of a level filled in turn by three muted pastel tints; no legend; each edge a line with its label; each node and edge one badge per status of the claims whose `on` names it, its symbol and count in its colour: `✓` green for those that hold, `?` grey for unknown ones, `!` orange for conflicting ones and `✗` red for those that fail, a node's inside its lower right corner, on its tag's line, and an edge's after its label, shown there while the label is hidden and kept there while the lit label moves; a level too full for the window is drawn denser, never with smaller type, and a label that would cover another thing shows only while its edge is lit by the pointer, a pick or a scenario, in a place free among what is lit. A node is never narrower than its label's longest word.
- **overlay**: the card beside the node, edge or area under the pointer, clear of that record and of what is tied to it, while the rest of the diagram steps back; it shows one row per item in the item's form, wraps a long text inside its width, and pages the rows its height cannot hold by its `More rows` button, which stays in place as the pages turn, with fewer rows a page where no such place holds more; it scrolls only round one row taller than itself, its button still in view; none comes while a level is still arriving. A click keeps the same card open; Escape closes it.
- **card words**: a card's texts explain themselves: a record named as `{id}` is a chip whose hover marks that record, or the node of the shown level that opens the way to it; the first match of each term is a term whose hover shows its meaning; `N sources` opens each cited source's path and `says`; a `(guess)` and a claim's `unknown`, `conflicting` or `fails` status are badges, a holding one none; a node's or an edge's card lists the claims its badges count under `holds`, `unknown`, `conflicting` and `fails`, so the pointer on a badge, or a click on it, shows them, and a click on a claim's chip opens its card; a quote is code, a fence a block keeping its lines, and a formula MathML that `make-page.py` drew, inline or as a centered block.
- **view**: a level, the record picked on it, and a scenario with its step; every move makes one, the address holds it as `#level=…&at=…&scenario=…&step=…`, and the browser's back and forward, or `[` and `]`, walk the views in order; a double click is one move, to the level its node opens or to the node picked. A move to another level is staged: the level left gives way, then the level entered grows out of the node that opens it or settles around the node that was left; a move turned back part-way goes on from the level still seen; under reduced motion both only fade, as does each part that shows.
- **wrapping**: every text wraps only between words, Korean ones too, over as many lines as it needs; only a single unbroken string wider than its whole box, such as a long address, breaks inside it.
- **scenario play**: `Scenarios` lists each scenario with its number of steps. A bar at the foot holds Previous step, Play or Pause, Next step, the count, the title, one button per step and Close; the arrow keys step, the space key plays the scenario by itself and stops it, and the step after the last offers `Replay` and `Next scenario`. A mark travels the step's edge; the path walked, the step and the record the next step goes to are each drawn apart from the rest, and a step's note, when it has one, sits beside its record, whole, however long under a `Step <n>` heading, with a bar in the scenario mark's colour that no edge label has.
- **search**: `/` opens it. A query matches a record's name or alias, id, tag, sentence and other texts, a term's alias read as the term: the whole text, a word, the start of a word, the first letters of its words, inside a word, then a word one or two slips away. The records that match every word, or for a query of three words or more every record sharing one other than a stop word such as `the`, are ranked by reciprocal rank fusion, k = 60, of four rankers: the field matched (name, id, tag, sentence, other texts), the match's quality in that order, BM25 over all texts, and the share of words matched. A query typed with the Korean two-set layout left on is read as the keys pressed. Each hit marks the letters matched, and when a word misses the name it names the field it matched and shows about twelve words around the match, as `matched alias: <alias>` for an alias, and as `matched alias: <alias> (<term>)` for a term's alias. The `.*` button or Alt+R turns the regular expression mode on: the whole input is one pattern, case ignored, with no slips or initials, and a bad pattern says `Not a regular expression` with its reason; a query nothing finds restates itself with a hint. A query a hit was picked from is kept with its mode, the last eight per contract title and file path, in `localStorage`, or for the tab where storage throws; opened empty the search lists them with a clock above the rebuild questions, and typing keeps those that start with it, above the hits, while Enter still opens the top hit; a recalled query comes back in its mode. A question, listed or found by its words, leads to the first record that answers it, or to the gap it is left to.

`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/examples/self/page.html` and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/examples/page-changes/page.html` are the pages of the two example contracts.

## Steps

1. Build: `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/scripts/make-page.py <contract> [<page>]`; over the page it rewrites, it checks and builds again only the parts whose key changed, a key covering the part, what it reads of the other parts and the code, keeps the rest, and prints both lists.
2. Hand the page's path to the person, who opens it in a desktop browser.
3. Only when the owner asks to take comments on it, connect and serve the page as [relay.md](relay.md) says; apply a comment to the contract, and build again.

## Rules

- **Code draws**: a change of look, layout or motion goes into [../assets/page.css](../assets/page.css) or [../assets/page.js](../assets/page.js), or, for the record card with its badges and the search, into the plugin's shared parts [record-card.css](../../../lib/record-card.css), [record-card.js](../../../lib/record-card.js), [record-search.css](../../../lib/record-search.css) and [record-search.js](../../../lib/record-search.js); a change of content goes into the contract; no page is edited by hand.
- **Layout**: each space in `page.css` and the shared parts' style is 0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 or 32 px; a group's parts sit at most half as far apart as the group sits from the next, a part stays inside its frame, a column's parts share one edge, and text reads at 4.5:1 or more; `run-page.cjs layout` measures each.
- **After a change to the page's code**: build each example's page again, then run `node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/scripts/run-page.cjs`, which must print `PASS`.
- **Before judging the look**: `node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/scripts/capture-page.cjs <folder>` writes pictures and measures of each example page; judge from those, as **Evaluation** in `AGENTS.md` says.
