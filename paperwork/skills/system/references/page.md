# page

Show a checked contract as one HTML page a person reads, searches and plays without leaving its diagram.

## Domain

- **page**: one `.html` file made by `make-page.py` from a contract. The contract is its page data, and each node, edge and area is an anchor, so it follows the page rules of the `relay` skill.
- **diagram**: one level drawn to fill the window: each node a rectangle with its label and its tag's name, each area a frame, each edge a line with its label; a level too full for the window is drawn denser, never with smaller type, and a label that would cover another thing shows only while its edge is lit by the pointer, a pick or a scenario, in a place free among what is lit.
- **overlay**: the card beside the node, edge or area under the pointer, clear of that record and of what is tied to it, while the rest of the diagram steps back; it shows one row per item in the item's form, wraps a long text inside its width, and pages the rows its height cannot hold by its `More rows` button, which stays in place as the pages turn, with fewer rows a page where no such place holds more; it scrolls only round one row taller than itself, its button still in view; none comes while a level is still arriving. A click keeps the same card open; Escape closes it.
- **card words**: a card's texts explain themselves: a record named as `{id}` is a chip whose hover marks that record, or the node of the shown level that opens the way to it; the first match of each term is a term whose hover shows its meaning; `N sources` opens each cited source's path and `says`; a `(guess)` and a property's `unknown` or `broken` status are badges, a holding one none; a quote is code, a fence a block keeping its lines, and a formula MathML that `make-page.py` drew, inline or as a centered block.
- **view**: a level, the record picked on it, and a scenario with its step; every move makes one, the address holds it as `#level=…&at=…&scenario=…&step=…`, and the browser's back and forward, or `[` and `]`, walk the views in order; a double click is one move, to the level its node opens or to the node picked. A move to another level is staged: the level left gives way, then the level entered grows out of the node that opens it or settles around the node that was left; a move turned back part-way goes on from the level still seen; under reduced motion both only fade, as does each part that shows.
- **scenario play**: `Scenarios` lists each scenario with its number of steps. A bar at the foot holds Previous step, Play or Pause, Next step, the count, the title, one button per step and Close; the arrow keys step, the space key plays the scenario by itself and stops it, and the step after the last offers `Replay` and `Next scenario`. A mark travels the step's edge; the path walked, the step and the record the next step goes to are each drawn apart from the rest, and a step's note, when it has one, sits beside its record.
- **search**: `/` opens it. A query matches a record's name, id, tag, sentence and other texts: the whole text, a word, the start of a word, the first letters of its words, inside a word, then a word one or two slips away. The records that match every word are ranked by reciprocal rank fusion, k = 60, of four rankers: the field matched (name, id, tag, sentence, other texts), the match's quality in that order, BM25 over all texts, and the share of words matched; a sentence of three words or more that no record matches in every word lists the records that share its words. A query typed with the Korean two-set layout left on is read as the keys pressed. Each hit marks the letters matched, and when a word misses the name it names the field it matched and shows about twelve words around the match. The `.*` button or Alt+R turns the regular expression mode on: the whole input is one pattern, case ignored, with no slips or initials, and a bad pattern says `Not a regular expression` with its reason; a query nothing finds restates itself with a hint. A query a hit was picked from is kept, the last eight per contract title and file path, in `localStorage`, or for the tab where storage throws; opened empty the search lists them with a clock above the rebuild questions, and typing keeps those that start with it, above the hits, while Enter still opens the top hit. A question, listed or found by its words, leads to the first record that answers it, or to the gap it is left to.

`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/examples/self/page.html` and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/examples/page-changes/page.html` are the pages of the two example contracts.

## Steps

1. Build: `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/scripts/make-page.py <contract> [<page>]`.
2. Hand the page's path to the person, who opens it in a desktop browser.
3. To take the person's comments on it, connect and serve the page as the `relay` skill says; apply a comment to the contract, and build again.

## Rules

- **Code draws**: a change of look, layout or motion goes into [../assets/page.css](../assets/page.css) or [../assets/page.js](../assets/page.js), and a change of content into the contract; no page is edited by hand.
- **After a change to the page's code**: build each example's page again, then run `node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/scripts/run-page.cjs`, which must print `PASS`.
- **Before judging the look**: `node ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/system/scripts/capture-page.cjs <folder>` writes pictures and measures of each example page; judge from those, as **Evaluation** in `AGENTS.md` says.
