# Manager succession

Hand a manager's work to its successor, or restart a peer manager.

## Steps

1. To hand over, `start` `<your name>-next` in your workspace with `succeed <your name>`, and stop. To restart a peer manager, at the owner's word or once a merge changed a monitor it runs, `start` `<peer>-next` at its cwd with `succeed <peer>` while it is idle, no issue needed. Started with `succeed <name>`: take only read-only steps, neither `rename` yourself nor prompt workers, until you have closed that session, asking the owner first when a peer asked for the close and at once when it is refused; then `rename` yourself `<name>`, and go to step 4 of [manager.md](manager.md); the monitor reprints every open gap once a worker lives.
