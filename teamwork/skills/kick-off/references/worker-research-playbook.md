# Research

1. Fan out at once one `sonnet` `medium` subagent per independent source set (transcripts, issues, official docs, the web), each brief naming its output form.
2. Write the report to a file: findings, each citing its source, then each criterion with its command, result and output on main; `comment` it on the issue as `REPORTED:`.
3. A `judge` reviews the comment's url; answer its gaps in a new comment, judged again, until it approves.
