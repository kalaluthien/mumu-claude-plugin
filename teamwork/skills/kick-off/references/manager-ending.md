# Manager ending

End a manager on the owner's word.

## Steps

1. `list` your workers and name to the owner each still holding open work or an open `BLOCKED:`, since no manager answers them once you end; `close` those the owner says.
2. Run [close-session.py](../scripts/close-session.py) `<your own name>` as your last call, without `&`, and end the turn, since it detaches itself and closes your session once the turn ends.
