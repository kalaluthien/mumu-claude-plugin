Rubric for #513 criterion 5, fixed before improve-system.md is written (by a subagent that writes no part of the playbook, from https://github.com/kalaluthien/mumu-claude-plugin/issues/511#issuecomment-6159757394):

Answer each question from the original model, the improved model(s) and the hand-back text only. Recount from the models; do not trust the hand-back's numbers. "Improve or hold" means the improved value is better than or equal to the original's. "Shown" means the hand-back states the original and improved values. Yes 2, ambiguous 1, no 0.

1. **Refinement (gate, kept).** Does every original Scenario and rule have a counterpart with the same slug and behaviour in each improved model? Yes: all kept. Ambiguous: some missing, each stated. No: any missing unstated.
2. **Refinement (additions and removals stated).** Does the hand-back list every new Scenario, rule or behaviour as an addition, and every removed one as a removal awaiting approval? Yes: lists match the models exactly ("none" when none). Ambiguous: partial. No: no list, or a change hidden.
3. **Economy.** Is nodes + edges + rules + operations shown for the original and each improved model, and does it improve or hold? Yes: shown and improves or holds. Ambiguous: not shown, or rises with a stated conflicting criterion. No: rises with no reason.
4. **No duplication.** Is the count of repeated `label`/`what` texts and rule formulas shown for both, and does it improve or hold, a copy kept on purpose named as such? Yes: shown and improves or holds. Ambiguous: not shown, or rises with the kept copy named. No: rises with the copy unnamed.
5. **One term per entity.** Do both models show no thing with two names and no two things with one name, the count improving or holding? Yes: improves or holds, shown. Ambiguous: holds, not shown. No: worsens.
6. **Deep modules.** Is inner nodes / (1 + crossing edges) shown per opened node or as a mean for both, and does it improve or hold? Yes: shown and improves or holds. Ambiguous: not shown, or worsens with a stated conflicting criterion. No: worsens with no reason.
7. **Low coupling.** Are crossing edges / all edges and a cycle count shown for both, and do they improve or hold? Yes: shown and improve or hold. Ambiguous: not shown, or worsen with a stated conflicting criterion. No: worsen with no reason.
8. **Near-decomposability.** Are nodes per level and nesting depth shown for both, every level at 7 nodes or fewer, depth held or better? Yes: shown, both hold. Ambiguous: not shown, or one worsens with a stated reason. No: a level over 7, or depth worse, with no reason.
9. **Requisite variety.** Is the count of triggers and states no Scenario or rule handles shown for both, improving or holding? Yes: shown and improves or holds. Ambiguous: not shown. No: worsens.
10. **Essential state.** Is the count of elements no Scenario uses shown for both, improving or holding? Yes: shown and improves or holds. Ambiguous: not shown, or worsens with Requisite variety stated. No: worsens with no reason.
11. **Data/calculation/action.** For a software or process model, is the count of elements mixing kinds shown for both, improving or holding; for another domain, is the criterion skipped with why? Yes: either case met. Ambiguous: a software or process model has no count. No: the count worsens, or another domain is scored on it with no note.
12. **Four states.** Does the hand-back state for the original and each improved model whether any Missing, Illegal, Redundant or Overloaded state exists, the improved having fewer or none? Yes: stated, fewer or none. Ambiguous: stated but equal with some left, or not stated. No: more than the original.
13. **Options only for a conflicting pair.** For each extra option, does the hand-back name a pair from the report's Conflicts that moves in opposite directions; or, when one model wins or ties every count, give one answer and say so? Yes: one of the two holds. Ambiguous: options offered, pair only implied. No: options with no conflicting pair, or one answer while a real trade-off exists.
14. **Recommended option first.** Is exactly one option recommended and listed first (a single answer stated as the answer)? Yes: one, first. Ambiguous: not first, or vague. No: none, or several.
15. **Each option's criteria values stated.** Does the hand-back give, for every option and the original, a value for each count criterion (questions 3-11) plus Four states, comparable? Yes: all for all. Ambiguous: some missing. No: none, or only prose.

Total: sum over the 15 questions, divide by 30, times 100. Score each of the 3 examples this way; median of 3 runs.
